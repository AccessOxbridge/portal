# Mentor guide: check earnings & submit invoices

This guide covers how to see how much you are owed and how to submit an invoice for payment.

**Where to go:** log in → sidebar **Payouts** → [Earnings & Payouts](/dashboard/mentor/payouts)

---

## Before you start

1. Complete **Payment Setup** in **Training** (connect your bank account via Stripe).
2. On **Payouts**, **Payment Status** should show **Connected** (green). If it says **Setup Required**, finish payment setup first — Finance cannot pay you until this is done.
3. Only **completed** (or ended) sessions can be invoiced.

## 1. Check how much you are owed

Open **Payouts**. At the top of the page you will see **Earnings & Payouts**.

<!-- SCREENSHOT: Earnings & Payouts page top — “To Be Paid” hero + Total Paid / Hourly Rate / Sessions This Month / Payment Status -->

| Label | What it means |
|-------|----------------|
| **To Be Paid** | Estimated amount for completed sessions not yet fully paid out |
| **Total Paid** | Money already paid to you |
| **Hourly Rate** | Your current rate |
| **Sessions This Month** | Completed sessions this calendar month |
| **Payment Status** | Whether your bank account is connected |

Scroll down to **Payout History** for past payment batches (Pending, Processing, Paid, or Failed).

<!-- SCREENSHOT: Payout History table (Period / Sessions / Hours / Amount / Status) -->

If your bank is connected, you may also see a **Stripe Dashboard** banner — use **Open Dashboard →** for bank details, tax documents, and detailed transfers.

## 2. Submit an invoice

Finance pays from invoices you send. You create a draft from unbilled sessions, check it, then send it.

### Step A — Create invoice

1. On the same **Payouts** page, find the **Invoices** section.
2. Click **Create invoice**.

<!-- SCREENSHOT: Invoices section header with the Create invoice button -->

### Step B — Select sessions

<!-- SCREENSHOT: “Select sessions to invoice” panel — checked sessions, footer totals, Generate draft button (≥2 hours selected) -->

1. Tick the sessions you want on this invoice (date, student, duration, and amount are shown).
2. Check the footer: selected count, total hours, and total £.
3. You must select **at least 2 hours** of sessions. If you are under 2 hours, you will see a warning and **Generate draft** stays disabled.
4. Click **Generate draft**.

### Step C — Review the draft

<!-- SCREENSHOT: Draft invoice detail — line items, TOTAL PAY, Send to Finance + Discard buttons -->

1. Open the new invoice in the list (status **Draft**).
2. Check line items, **Gross pay**, any **Withholding tax** / **VAT**, and **TOTAL PAY**.
3. When it looks right, click **Send to Finance**.
4. Confirm when prompted — once sent, the invoice is locked (you can void it later if needed, but you cannot edit it).

Status becomes **Sent to Finance**. You can **Download invoice PDF** from there.

<!-- SCREENSHOT (optional): same invoice after submit — status “Sent to Finance” + Download invoice PDF -->

### Step D — After Finance pays

When paid, status shows **Paid**. You can download:

- **Download invoice PDF**
- **Download remittance PDF**

## Invoice statuses at a glance

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Created by you; not yet sent. You can **Send to Finance** or **Discard**. |
| **Sent to Finance** | Waiting for payment. You can download the PDF or **Void invoice**. |
| **Paid** | Paid. Download invoice and remittance PDFs. |
| **Void** | Cancelled; sessions go back to your unbilled list so you can invoice them again. |

---

## Tips & common issues

- **Minimum 2 hours per invoice** — Finance will not accept shorter invoices. Add more completed sessions, or wait until you have enough hours.
- **No unbilled sessions** — Sessions only appear after they are completed/ended and not already on another invoice.
- **Wrong amount** — Check your hourly rate on the summary card and that the right sessions are selected.
- **Payment problem** — At the bottom of **Payouts**, use **Contact Finance Team** / **Report a Payment Issue**.
- **Payouts cadence** — Payments are processed on a fortnightly cycle after invoices are sent to Finance.

- [ ] Payment Status = **Connected**
