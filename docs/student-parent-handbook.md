# Student & Parent Portal Handbook

This guide explains how students use the Access Oxbridge portal day to day — from first login through booking sessions, joining Zoom, watching recordings, and reading reports. Parents have a short section at the end.

**Portal:** [https://app.accessoxbridge.io](https://app.accessoxbridge.io)

**Who this is for**

| Audience | How they use the portal |
|----------|-------------------------|
| **Students** | Log in and use the full student dashboard |
| **Parents / guardians** | No separate login today — receive fortnightly progress emails when a parent email is on the student’s profile |

Screenshots below will appear once images are added under [`assets/student-handbook/`](assets/student-handbook/). See [CAPTURE.md](assets/student-handbook/CAPTURE.md) for the capture checklist.

## 1. Getting access & logging in

Your account is created for you by the Access Oxbridge team. There is no public self-signup.

1. Open the portal and go to **Login**.
2. Enter the email and temporary password you were given (or the password you set later).
3. You land on your student **Home**.

![Login page](assets/student-handbook/01-login.png)

**Forgot your password?** On the login page, use **Forgot password**, enter your email, and follow the reset link we send you.

**Where to go:** [/login](/login) · Forgot password: [/forgot-password](/forgot-password)

---

## 2. Home overview

After login you see **Home** (`/dashboard/student`).

![Student home with Book a session](assets/student-handbook/02-home.png)

Use the left sidebar:

| Item | What it’s for |
|------|----------------|
| **Home** | Greeting, profile summary, weekly calendar, upcoming sessions |
| **My Sessions** | Pending, upcoming, current, and past sessions |
| **Recordings** | Rewatch past Zoom sessions |
| **Messages** | Chat with your mentors and Help & Support |
| **My Mentors** | Mentors assigned to you |
| **Reports** | Session progress reports |
| **Book a session** | Start a new booking (also on Home) |

Also look for:

- **Hours** floating button — how many session hours you have left
- **Help & Support** — message the team if something is stuck
- Profile menu (top) — **My Profile**, **Settings**, **Sign Out**

---

## 3. Complete your academic profile

You need a complete academic profile before you can book.

1. Open **My Profile** from the profile menu, or go to `/dashboard/student/profile`.
2. Fill in school, curriculum, subjects/grades, targets, interests, and **timezone**.
3. Optionally add a **Parent/guardian email** so parents can receive progress emails.
4. Click **Save Profile**.

![Academic profile](assets/student-handbook/03-profile.png)

Tip: use a real timezone name such as `Europe/London`, `America/New_York`, or `Asia/Dubai`. Session times in the portal follow this timezone.

**Where to go:** [/dashboard/student/profile](/dashboard/student/profile)

---

## 4. Your mentors

Mentors are assigned by the Access Oxbridge team. You do not pick mentors from a public directory.

1. Open **My Mentors** in the sidebar.
2. Check your current (and any past) mentors.

![My Mentors](assets/student-handbook/04-mentors.png)

If no mentor is listed yet, wait for the team to assign one — booking stays unavailable until then. Message **Help & Support** if you think an assignment is missing.

**Where to go:** [/dashboard/student/mentors](/dashboard/student/mentors)

## 5. Session hours (credits)

Each booked hour of mentoring uses **1 session hour** (credit).

- The floating **Hours** button shows your remaining balance.
- When you’re low or out of hours, click it and **Request more hours** — a message goes to the team.
- You may also buy packages (when offered) under **Services**.

![Session hours / packages](assets/student-handbook/10-credits-services.png)

**Where to go:** [/dashboard/student/services](/dashboard/student/services)

---

## 6. Book a session

Before booking, make sure you have:

1. A **complete** academic profile  
2. At least one **assigned mentor**  
3. Enough **session hours**

### Steps

1. Click **Book a session** in the sidebar (or the equivalent CTA on Home).
2. In the **Book a Session** dialog:
   - If you have more than one mentor, choose who to **Book with**.
   - Pick a **Date**, **Start Time**, and **End Time** (1 or 2 hours).
   - Times are shown in your saved timezone.
3. Submit the request. Your mentor confirms the time.

![Book a Session dialog](assets/student-handbook/05-book-session.png)

Your request appears under **My Sessions → Pending** until the mentor accepts. After acceptance it moves to **Upcoming**.

## 7. My Sessions — join, reschedule, cancel

Open **My Sessions**.

![My Sessions with Join and Reschedule](assets/student-handbook/06-sessions.png)

| Tab | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| **Pending** | Booking or reschedule requests waiting for your mentor |
| **Upcoming** | Confirmed sessions not started yet |
| **Current** | Sessions happening now |
| **Past** | Finished sessions |

### Join a session (Zoom)

1. Open **Upcoming** or **Current**.
2. When it’s time, click **Join Session**.
3. **Important:** your mentor must click **Start Session** first. If you join before they start, Zoom will keep you waiting for the host.

You will also get reminders before the session (email and/or in-app). Zoom links are opened from the portal — not emailed as standalone join links.

### Reschedule

On an **Upcoming** session, click **Reschedule**, propose a new time, and wait for your mentor to accept. While a reschedule is pending, you’ll see that status on the session.

### Cancel a pending request

On the **Pending** tab you can cancel requests you started (for example **Cancel my pending requests**). This cancels pending booking/reschedule *requests*, not a separate “cancel confirmed live session” policy screen.

**Where to go:** [/dashboard/student/sessions](/dashboard/student/sessions)

## 8. Watch recordings

After a session, the Zoom cloud recording is processed and then appears in the portal (this can take a little while after the meeting ends).

1. Open **Recordings** in the sidebar, **or**
2. On **My Sessions → Past**, use **Watch Recording** when available.

![Recordings list](assets/student-handbook/07-recordings.png)

Click a recording to play it in the portal. Hovering a card may show a short preview.

**Where to go:** [/dashboard/student/recordings](/dashboard/student/recordings)

---

## 9. Session reports & feedback

### Reports

After a session, a progress report is prepared from the session (and mentor input). When ready:

1. Open **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Read your session reports and follow up with your mentor via **Messages** if needed.

![Reports](assets/student-handbook/08-reports.png)

Reports can take time after the session (recording/transcript processing). If nothing appears after a day, contact **Help & Support**.

### Feedback

After a session ends, from **Past** sessions you can **Leave Feedback** so the team knows how it went.

**Where to go:** [/dashboard/student/reports](/dashboard/student/reports)

## 11. Settings

1. Open the profile menu → **Settings**.
2. Update your **timezone** and click **Save timezone**.
3. To change your password, use **Send password reset link** — we’ll email a secure link to your account email.

![Settings](assets/student-handbook/11-settings.png)

**Where to go:** [/dashboard/settings](/dashboard/settings)

## 12. For parents

Parents and guardians **do not have a separate portal login** today.

What you can expect:

- **Fortnightly progress emails** when a parent/guardian email is saved on the student’s academic profile.
- Your child (or an admin) can set or update that address under **My Profile → Parent/guardian email**.

What your student can do in the portal:

- Complete their profile and keep timezone accurate  
- Book and join sessions with assigned mentors  
- Message mentors and the support team  
- Rewatch recordings and read session reports  
- Request or purchase more session hours when needed  

If you’re not receiving emails, ask your student to check the parent email on their profile, and check spam/junk folders.

## Tips & common issues

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| **Can’t book** | Complete your profile, confirm a mentor is assigned under **My Mentors**, and check you have session hours left. |
| **Out of hours** | Use the **Hours** button → request more hours, or open **Services** to buy a package if available. |
| **Stuck “waiting for the host” in Zoom** | Your mentor needs to **Start Session** first; then use **Join Session**. |
| **No recording yet** | Wait for Zoom processing after the session; then check **Recordings** or **Watch Recording** on Past sessions. |
| **No report yet** | Reports appear after processing and mentor input — check **Reports** later, or message Help & Support. |
| **Wrong times on the calendar** | Set the correct timezone in **Profile** and **Settings**. |
| **Password issues** | Use **Forgot password** on login, or **Send password reset link** in Settings. |
| **Need human help** | Use **Help & Support** (floating button or Messages). |
